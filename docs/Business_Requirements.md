# Business Requirements

## Project Name
AI-Powered Supply Chain Decision Copilot

## Company Scenario
GlobalMart Retail Corporation is a simulated retail and e-commerce company with multiple warehouses, suppliers, products, purchase orders, shipments, and demand forecasts.

## Business Problem
Supply chain analysts spend significant time manually checking inventory reports, supplier performance, purchase orders, shipment delays, demand forecasts, and procurement documents.

The goal of this project is to create an AI-powered decision support system that allows users to ask supply chain questions in natural language and receive accurate, data-driven insights.

## Target Users
- Supply Chain Analysts
- Inventory Analysts
- Procurement Analysts
- Demand Planning Analysts
- Operations Managers

## Example Questions
- Which products are below safety stock?
- Which suppliers have poor on-time delivery performance?
- Which purchase orders are delayed?
- Which products should be reordered this week?
- What is the emergency procurement policy?
- What does the supplier contract say about late shipment penalties?

## Main Features
1. Natural language supply chain Q&A
2. Inventory analysis
3. Supplier performance analysis
4. Purchase order delay tracking
5. Demand forecast review
6. Document search using RAG
7. AI-powered recommendations
